# ***🧪 Pre-Lab Challenges***

As I mentioned in the main README of this repository, this section is intended to provide three basic exercises designed to be completed before each day of the course. The idea is simple: give you a solid base so that when you go into the actual training, you are not starting from zero.

These are not official labs, and they are not meant to replicate them. Instead, they are small, self-contained exercises that you can do on your own setup, without needing any of the hardware devices. They are purely software-oriented and focused on helping you build the mindset and the initial context needed for each day.

Inside this folder, you will find three subfolders:

- Day 1
- Day 2
- Day 3

Each one contains a single exercise that I believe is useful to complete before that specific day of the course.

As I said before, the goal is not to disclose or reproduce any of the official SANS labs, that's not possible due to copyright, and it's not the intention anyway.

What I'm trying to do here is give you a starting point so that when you face the real labs, you already have some familiarity with the concepts and workflows involved.

Also, if you are wondering what specific labs are included in the course, SANS actually lists them in the **[official course syllabus](https://www.sans.org/cyber-security-courses/iot-penetration-testing#syllabus)** on their website. If you expand the detailed description ("more"), you can see exactly which labs are planned for each day.

For this specific course, those labs are outlined there, and these exercises are meant to complement that by helping you arrive better prepared.

That's it, keep it simple, do the exercises, and you'll get much more out of the course.
